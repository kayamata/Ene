I think it's time we start finding our path. Not necessarily having every answer right now, but making a conscious effort to understand what we want, where we're going, and how we can get there together.

The names we've given ourselves along the way don't really matter that much. What matters is the journey, the lessons, the growth, and what we choose to make of this next chapter.

And honestly, for a long-distance couple, I think we're really trying. We're not perfect, but we're learning.
